Tiling

Tiling around a vertex

Class meeting · What fits around one point?

Draw a point and imagine placing regular polygons around it. A triangle contributes 60°, a square 90°, a hexagon 120°, an octagon 135°, and a dodecagon 150°.

Before class: Find two combinations that add to 360°. Sketch one and see how many different orders you can give its shapes around the point.

Construction

Compass and straightedge

Class meeting · Make a pattern from twelve marks

Use the circle as a starting point. Connect any marks that interest you, draw arcs, or use new intersections to build up your drawing.

Before class: Bring your drawing and be ready to describe one choice you made.
